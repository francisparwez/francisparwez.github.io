About Me

I'm a Data Analyst with an MSc in Data Science with Advanced Research from the University of Hertfordshire and a BSc in Computer Science. My work sits at the intersection of data analysis, business intelligence, data quality, ETL, and practical machine learning.

I began my professional career as a MERN Stack Developer at Divine Virtuality, building responsive web applications and REST APIs for international clients. That software background gave me a strong foundation in programming, databases, APIs, Git, debugging, and structured problem solving.

Today, I work across the analytics pipeline — auditing raw data, cleaning and transforming it, exploring patterns, building KPIs, developing dashboards, and documenting the work so it can be understood and reproduced. My current Data Science & Analytics internship at BeeNeural adds hands-on experience with Python, Pandas, NumPy, visualization, feature engineering, and statistical analysis.
