About Me

I started out writing code, not charts. A year as a MERN stack developer at Divine Virtuality had me building React front ends and Node.js APIs for clients across the US, India, and Lebanon — and it's where I learned to care about whether software actually works for the person using it, not just whether it compiles.

That instinct pulled me toward data. I went on to complete an MSc in Data Science (with Advanced Research) at the University of Hertfordshire, and now split my time between Power BI dashboards, SQL queries, and Python notebooks — figuring out what a dataset is actually trying to tell a business, then building something a non-technical stakeholder can look at and immediately understand.

I'm just as comfortable writing a DAX measure as I am debugging a React component, and I like it that way — it means I can take a problem from "here's a messy spreadsheet" all the way to "here's a dashboard you can act on."
